## 1. Padmasambhava (Guru Rinpoche) (`1024x682`)

* **Subtitle**: *Compassion · Wisdom · Courage — A Brighter World*
* **Core Quotes**:
  * *"May all beings be happy."*
  * *"A kinder heart creates a brighter world."*
* **Roles**: *Kind Teacher · Wisdom Guide · Protector of All Beings · Bringer of Peace*
* **Official Color Palette**:
  * Monastic Crimson: `#9E2A2B` (`Color(0.62, 0.16, 0.17)`)
  * Sacred Gold: `#DCA03A` (`Color(0.86, 0.63, 0.23)`)
  * Celestial Indigo: `#1E386B` (`Color(0.12, 0.22, 0.42)`)
  * Silk Ivory: `#F3EAD8` (`Color(0.95, 0.92, 0.85)`)
  * Peacock Teal: `#3B6E75` (`Color(0.23, 0.43, 0.46)`)
* **Key Details**:
  1. **Lotus Hat (Utpala)** — Crowned with a peacock feather and sun-moon symbol representing enlightened view.
  2. **Vajra** — *Symbol of Wisdom* (indestructible compassion and clarity).
  3. **Lotus Flower** — *Purity* (unfolding unstained amidst worldly suffering).
  4. **Khatvanga Staff** — *Transformation* (pacifying the three poisons of ignorance, attachment, and aversion).
  5. **Robe Pattern** — *Compassion for All Beings* (gold-embroidered lotus brocade).
  6. **Boots** — *For the Journey* (steadfast travel across mountains to bring peace).
* **Sprite Slice Coordinates (`1024x682`)**:
  * `guru_portrait.png`: `(11, 111, 177, 264)`
